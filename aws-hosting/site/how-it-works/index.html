How Scoutline works

A clearer way to check a scam.

Scoutline collects trustworthy public warnings, removes duplicate reports, and turns useful guidance into plain language.

1 · Collect

Trusted sources

We monitor official agencies and established nonprofit fraud-warning organizations.

2 · Verify

Signals are not facts

Community reports are not published as fact without corroboration.

3 · Simplify

Clear next steps

Each guide explains what is happening, the warning signs, and what to do next.

4 · Share

Practical safety kits

Turn guidance into printable and shareable materials for the people around you.
